Three layouts for the travel stories

All three tell the story in first person, lead with large photos and keep the route on the right: highlights, tips and a slot for the map. Photos are stand-ins and the text is condensed from the Bosnia & Slovenia draft.

A

Journal

Full-width hero, the story in chapters with big photos in between. Route card sticks along while you read.

  • Most like a travel magazine
  • Best for long, personal stories
  • Map and stops stay in view

B

Stop by stop

The story is split into numbered stops, each with a photo and a tip. The route list on the right lights up as you read.

  • Most structured and easiest to scan
  • Grows into a clickable map
  • Good for tips per place

Photo first
