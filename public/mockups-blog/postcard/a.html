Trip no. 1

Maiden trip: Bosnia and Slovenia

By Edwin · 3 to 20 August 2025 · family with dog · 9 min read

In shortOur first overland trip ever. We planned Norway, turned south a few days before leaving and spent almost two weeks in Bosnia: vineyards, rafting in Una, wild horses near Livno, gravel to Lukomir and a hike into Montenegro.

Planned Norway, drove south

Just a few days before departure we were still undecided where to go. Norway had been the plan for our maiden trip, but the rugged camping and unpredictable weather gave us pause. Then Albania came up. One look at the map and we decided to just go for it.
