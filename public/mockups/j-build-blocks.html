Building blocks

Six blocks, one truck

Every build starts with the base for your vehicle. The other blocks work the same on a pickup and an SUV.

Always included
BLOCK A

Canopy

The base on a pickup: everything else mounts on or in it.

  • Aluminium canopy on the load bed
  • LED lighting inside
  • Ready for a roof tent on top

Roof rack & boot layout

The base on an SUV: a strong roof and a smart boot. [check]

  • Load-rated roof rack
  • Boot floor or drawer frame
  • LED lighting in the boot
Photo after our first SUV build
BLOCK B

Power

Off-grid for days, charged while you drive.

  • Dual battery system
  • Solar charging
  • USB and 12V outlets, pre-wired for later
BLOCK C

Sleep

Up on the roof, set up in minutes.

  • Roof tent(s), one or two
  • 270° awning with LED lights
  • On the roof rack from block A
  • On top of the canopy
BLOCK D

Kitchen & storage

Everything has a place, nothing rattles.

  • Drawer system in the load bedin the boot
  • Slide-out kitchen with gas stove
  • Fridge-ready, 35 or 45 L
BLOCK E

Water

Cook, wash up and shower anywhere.

  • Tank and pump
  • Basin for cooking and washing
  • Outside shower
BLOCK F

Off-road

Gets you there, and back out again.

  • Upgraded suspension
  • Winch, recovery boards, snorkel
  • Skid plates and rock sliders
  • Front and rear auxiliary lights
