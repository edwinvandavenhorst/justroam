Warranty

If something fails because of how we built it, or because of a part we supplied, we fix it at no cost. That applies for 12 months after delivery. Parts also carry their own manufacturer warranty, and we handle that claim for you.

Covered

  • Mistakes in our build and installation
  • Faulty parts we supplied

Not covered

  • Damage from use the build is not meant for, overloading or accidents
  • Changes or repairs by someone else
  • Normal wear
