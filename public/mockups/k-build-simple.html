How a build works

From first call to first trip

Four steps, and you know where you stand at every one of them.

  1. 01

    Intake

    A free 45-minute call about your vehicle, how you travel and your budget.

  2. 02

    Design together

    We design your interior around how you travel, and agree on price and planning.

  3. 03

    Build

    We build it, or you build alongside us. You choose.

  4. 04

    Handover

    We walk you through everything, and off you go.

Step 03, option 1

We build

You drop off your vehicle, we build it and keep you posted along the way. You pick it up ready to go.

Step 03, option 2

Build together

You build alongside us on agreed days. You learn how everything works, and you pay less for labour. We always do the electrics and the gas.

Plan your intake

A free, no-obligation 45-minute call, by phone or video. Together we go through:

  1. Your vehicle: make, model and year
  2. How you travel: weekends or weeks, with whom
  3. What you want, and what you are unsure about
  4. Your budget, and when the vehicle should be ready

Afterwards you know what is possible, roughly what it costs and how long it takes.
